1 Guardrails

10 items: 10 ticked. The design under all ten: the language model reads a customer's message and fills in nine fields, and may be asked which of the guide sections that were found fits. It has no tool. Code decides identity, network status, eligibility, amounts and bookings, and customers are sent templates.

    4.6Caching and scaling approach for peak load, with load-test result

    Evidence.     Evidence. Decision record: docs/adr/0001-degraded-mode-when-the-network-api-fails.md. Measured In 8 of 8 playings of the failure drill, 72 contacts, every rule held (evals/reports/curveball.md).
