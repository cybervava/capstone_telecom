Today. The network operations centre (NOC) knows of a fibre cut or a tower fault within minutes. Customer care is not told. Thousands of customers call, and agents take each one through the same reboot script. Three calls in ten end with a technician sent to a home where nothing is wrong. A repeat complaint within 7 days is SkyLink's top churn signal, and the regulator tracks how long complaints take to resolve.

What the sponsor wants. The Chief Customer Officer wants care to "know what the network knows", to resolve outage-related contacts automatically, and to offer fair service credits consistently.

3 Scope in and scope out

Broadband first, with a thin mobile path. Broadband carries the field cost that can be measured and has a fixed address-to-node mapping; the brief lists it as supplied, with guides for 12 router models. On mobile, a tower outage is still told, ticketed, restored and credited. The replay bears the order out: every missed detection among its 140 contacts was mobile, which has no remote test.

In scope: built, on mock APIs Built

  • The brief's six capabilities, for broadband: triage, diagnostics with a remote line test, steps from the customer's own router guide, credit by the policy formula in code, dispatch, consent-checked notification.
  • A complaint reference for every contact, chat or call transcript, issued before any model or dependency is called.
  • The human gate. A supervisor approves the exact credit above INR 500; up to INR 500 it is applied without review (client decision D3, 23 November 2026). Tests and evaluations run with every credit under review.
  • Safe degraded mode when the Network Monitoring API fails. Mobile, thin: a tower outage only.

Out of scope or deferred, and why Not built

  • Live telephony or IVR, real SMS, production OSS/BSS writes. The capstone runs against mock APIs only.
  • Mobile beyond the outage path. Static mapping; live tower association is a separate question of data readiness.
  • A churn model. No record of who left, and treatment by predicted churn risks unfair offers by segment. A repeat-contact rule only moves a case up the staff queue.
  • Credits for affected customers who did not contact SkyLink. Open client decision (D3, second part).
  • Metrics, dashboards, alerts; production hardening. Deferred (milestone M5 and after); the demonstration is one AWS host.
  • Fault prediction, repair, technician routing. Outside customer care.

Broadband first: workshop decision D2; action plan p.1. In and out: README.md, "Works today" and "Not built yet"; discovery report, section 5; docs/EXECUTION_PLAN.md, A10 and section 12.

What the curveball changed. A network status that cannot be read became a third answer, unknown, and is never "clear": the reference is still issued at once, no device steps and no visit are offered, and a person takes the contact after about five minutes (ADR 0001, accepted 16 November 2026). The price is a full staff queue in a long failure: 2,693 of 3,361 such contacts reached a person in the half-hour drill.
